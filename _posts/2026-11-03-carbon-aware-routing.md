## Key Idea

Papers in the past have focused on energy efficient networks, but few
have considered environmental impact. This paper aims to develop an
algorithm for carbon-aware routing. The algorithm, CATE, is a heuristic
algoorithm that uses carbon intensity and router energy measurements to
accomplish this task.

## Critique

Not so much a criticism, but I would be curious to see how this paper
holds up under the ongoing AI boom. Are the benefits of carbon aware
routing miniscule now? A lot has changed since being published in
2023, I would like to see work on the bigger picture on "green"
networking/computing today.

## Connections

Once again, like in _Unlocking ECMP Programmability for Precise Traffic Control_
or _Raha: A General Tool to Analyze WAN Degradation_, we come across a paper on
traffic engineering. However, unlike those previous papers, this one doesn't
optimize for speed or reliability. Instead, the paper is solving for a different
problem beyond efficiency, which makes it stand out in that regard.
